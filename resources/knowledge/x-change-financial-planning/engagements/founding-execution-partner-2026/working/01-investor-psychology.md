# Investor Psychology

## Purpose

Preserve the likely concerns, motivations, and decision logic of the ₱1.5M backer.

## Investor's Likely Question

The backer may not primarily be asking whether x-change can become a huge company.

She may be asking:

"If I provide ₱1.5M today, what is the safest path for me to get it back, and what milestones will increase the likelihood of that happening?"

## Implication

The proposal should not begin with world domination or hypergrowth.

It should begin with risk reduction, asset creation, and commercial validation.

## What She Needs to See

- Clear use of funds
- Tangible deliverables
- Practical cash-flow possibilities
- Bridge revenue options
- Recovery paths
- Evidence that the founder can execute
- Evidence that customers will pay

## Tone

The tone should be practical, disciplined, and grounded.

Avoid exaggerated forecasts.

Avoid presenting the ₱1.5M as if it alone proves the entire long-term x-change platform.

## Depends on Concepts

- Execution Capital
- Bridge Revenue
- Proof of Execution
- Survivability Ratio

## Supported by Evidence

- pilot-estimates
- implementation-costs
- salary-benchmarks
