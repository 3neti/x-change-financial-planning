# Engagement Overview

## Purpose

Define the funding engagement for a proposed ₱1.5M execution-capital backer.

## Core Thesis

The backer should be invited to become a Founding Execution Partner.

The capital should help x-change become commercially real by funding productization, proof of execution, and early paying validation.

## What This Engagement Is

- A proof-of-execution funding conversation
- A commercialization bridge
- A risk-reduction program
- A path toward tangible commercial assets
- A path toward early customer validation

## What This Engagement Is Not

- A hypergrowth fundraising round
- A generic startup pitch
- A finished financial model
- A guarantee of repayment
- A substitute for legal or investment structuring advice

## Main Questions

- What exactly will the ₱1.5M fund?
- What will exist after the funding period that does not exist today?
- How will progress be measured?
- How can the backer recover capital or participate in future success?
- What bridge revenue can be generated while waiting for larger funding or institutional adoption?

## Depends on Concepts

- Execution Capital
- Bridge Revenue
- Proof of Execution
- Incubation
