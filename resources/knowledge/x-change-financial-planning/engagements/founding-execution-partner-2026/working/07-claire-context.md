# Claire Context

## Purpose

Preserve the relational and financial context around Claire's possible ₱1.5M participation.

## Relationship Context

Claire is not a cold investor.

She is the current romantic partner of Mike, a very good friend.

She joined DBP meetings with Mike partly so Lester would not appear alone.

She attended two DBP meetings and was impressed.

## Personal Context

Claire is a balikbayan, New Yorker, divorced, and wants to live in the Philippines.

She recently bought a condominium.

## Funding Context

The ₱1.5M is expensive for her because she may be dipping into trust funds with a 25% penalty if not mature.

She is already convinced enough by the project to consider the risk.

She may be willing to write it off if the venture fails, but this should not be abused or leaned on.

## Strategic Implication

The proposal should feel like stewardship, not hype.

This is not ordinary fundraising distance.

It is a trust-based decision that deserves unusual care.
