# Bridge Revenue and Recovery

## Purpose

Preserve the emerging recovery logic for the ₱1.5M execution-capital backer.

## Core Issue

The prospective backer wants to understand:

- how she may recover the ₱1.5M when larger funding arrives
- whether interim cash flow can be generated while waiting
- whether the venture can reduce financing risk through practical commercial activity

## Bridge Revenue

Bridge Revenue is revenue that helps finance execution before long-term platform revenue matures.

It should not be confused with Platform Revenue.

Examples may include:

- paid pilots
- implementation services
- integration services
- custom deployment work
- enterprise automation
- consulting related to x-change capabilities
- early customer subscriptions
- support contracts

## Platform Revenue

Platform Revenue is the long-term scalable revenue model.

Examples may include:

- institutional licensing
- implementation fees
- settlement instruction fees
- recurring support
- treasury services
- AI services
- compliance services
- future platform modules

## Recovery Paths to Explore

Possible recovery paths may include:

- repayment from a later funding round
- repayment from bridge revenue
- conversion into equity or economic participation
- revenue share from early deployments
- participation in future commercial contracts
- structured founder/company obligation subject to legal advice

Do not represent any recovery path as guaranteed.

The structure requires legal, tax, and investment advice.

## Current Strategic Framing

The capital should be presented as funding a proof-of-execution program.

It buys risk reduction.

It should produce:

- commercial assets
- customer validation
- reference deployment
- stronger funding position
