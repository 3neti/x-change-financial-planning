# Founder Story

## Purpose

Preserve Lester's entrepreneurial history as context and lessons learned, not as a resume.

## Track Record

Lester has had five startups.

At 27, he had a small software and networking company, Gendunken Corporation.

Gendunken was bought for ₱10M in 1997.

He was absorbed by Platinum Plans, a large pre-need company.

He became President and CEO until around 2000.

## Later Ventures

He later pursued a large dot-com / internet backbone idea.

That venture had approved funding of ₱350M and received ₱35M to start.

A sudden political change occurred when the Philippine president was ousted, and investors reneged on the remaining 90%.

He ventured into four more startups.

The last was a community Wi-Fi project with ₱20M funding that went belly up due to internal and market issues.

## Why x-change Is Different

x-change is different because the core assets are more controllable:

- idea
- IP
- code
- productization
- AI-assisted execution

## Meaning

The lesson is not "this cannot fail."

The lesson is: this venture is built on lessons paid for over decades.
