# Legacy and Movement

## Purpose

Preserve the deeper purpose framing behind the execution-capital conversation.

## Founder Intention

If x-change does not become exactly what everyone hoped, Lester would still want to say the investment spawned a movement around the idea.

Success includes making a difference in the sphere of influence.

## Framing Discipline

"Movement" should be grounded, not grandiose.

"Sphere of influence" may be a better phrase for investor-facing writing.

## Investor Meaning

The final proposal should not only describe financial upside, but also the story Claire may tell herself later:

- she helped bring an important idea into the world
- she helped commercialize Pay Code
- she helped x-change make a difference in its reachable sphere
