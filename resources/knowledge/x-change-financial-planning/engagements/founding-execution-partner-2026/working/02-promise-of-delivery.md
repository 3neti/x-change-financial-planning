# Promise of Delivery

## Purpose

Define what the founder can credibly promise to deliver if the ₱1.5M is provided.

## Initial Promise

If funded, the intended delivery promise is:

- x-change package fully productized
- x-Payout fully productized
- brands completed
- patent/IP work advanced
- ready for commercialization to banks, SMEs, and high-net-worth individuals
- 3-5 paying companies using the platform

Investor roadshows were initially considered but may be removed from the core promise because another party may already be handling investor outreach.

## Refined Pillars

The delivery promise should be organized under two pillars.

### Pillar 1 - Build Commercial Assets

Deliver:

- x-change platform productized
- x-Payout productized
- commercial branding
- patent/IP strengthened
- deployment-ready documentation
- repeatable implementation methodology

### Pillar 2 - Validate the Business

Deliver:

- 3-5 paying companies
- production deployments
- operating cash flow
- customer references
- evidence that companies will pay

## Deliberately Deprioritized

Growth pipeline activity should not be the core promise unless it directly supports recovery, validation, or follow-on funding.

The current working view is:

The ₱1.5M is not primarily to prove fundraising capability.

It is to prove that x-change is commercially real.
