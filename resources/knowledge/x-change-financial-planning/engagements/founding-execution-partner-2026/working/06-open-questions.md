# Open Questions

## Purpose

Track unresolved questions before the ₱1.5M proposal becomes investor-facing.

## Investor Relationship

- Should the backer be treated as a lender, investor, founding execution partner, revenue-share participant, or some hybrid?
- What relationship does the founder want the backer to have with x-change long term?
- Should the backer participate only in repayment, or also in upside?

## Recovery Structure

- How exactly can the ₱1.5M be recovered?
- Is repayment triggered by follow-on funding?
- Is repayment supported by bridge revenue?
- Is conversion to equity possible or desirable?
- What legal instrument is appropriate?

## Execution Scope

- What is the minimum credible productization scope for x-change?
- What is the minimum credible productization scope for x-Payout?
- What patent/IP milestones are realistic within the funding period?
- What branding and commercialization assets must exist?

## Commercial Validation

- What counts as a paying company?
- What counts as "using it"?
- Are paid pilots enough?
- Are production transactions required?
- How many paying customers are realistic?

## IFX Validation

- Does IFX actually experience peso shortages?
- How often?
- Why?
- What do customers do when this happens?
- How much revenue is lost?
- Would IFX accept digital settlement as a solution?
- Which settlement rails would IFX trust?
- Would customers accept GCash, Maya, or bank transfer instead of cash?

## Settlement Rail Trust

- Which settlement partners would early merchants trust?
- Is NetBank sufficient?
- Is Paynamics sufficient?
- Is DBP necessary for broader credibility?
- How should x-change present rail flexibility?

## Bridge Revenue

- Which bridge-revenue activities are acceptable?
- Which activities distract from platform productization?
- What near-term customers can pay?
- What services can be sold without compromising the long-term platform?

## Budget

- How should the ₱1.5M be allocated?
- How much goes to productization?
- How much goes to patent/IP?
- How much goes to branding?
- How much goes to pilots?
- How much goes to operations?
- How much runway does it provide?

## Presentation

- Should the first output be a funding memorandum rather than a deck?
- What should be shown to the backer first?
- How much technical detail is appropriate?
