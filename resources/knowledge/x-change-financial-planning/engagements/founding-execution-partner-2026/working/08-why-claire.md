# Why Claire

## Purpose

Preserve why Claire is part of this funding conversation and why the role is more relational than transactional.

## Why She Is In Scope

Claire was not chosen merely because she can afford ₱1.5M.

Mike believes in Lester and wants to be part of the idea.

Mike may see the opportunity and wants to be involved in ODTI business development or downstream Pay Code integration.

Claire cares for Mike.

Mike's trust in Lester matters.

## Why She Saw the Work

Lester wanted Claire to see that x-change and Pay Code are real before asking.

Claire saw DBP meetings firsthand.

This means her interest is grounded in observed seriousness, not only secondhand storytelling.

## Human Framing

Claire may articulate the human side of Pay Code better than Lester, who naturally thinks as a developer and architect.

That perspective may be strategically important in later writing.

## Emerging Role

The emerging role is not merely "investor" but "Founding Execution Partner."
