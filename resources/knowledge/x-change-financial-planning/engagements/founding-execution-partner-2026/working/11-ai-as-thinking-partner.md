# AI as Thinking Partner

## Purpose

Preserve how AI is used in the founder's execution and decision discipline.

## Core View

Lester used AI to check, double-check, and challenge ideas.

AI is not framed as replacing the founder.

AI is a thinking partner, reviewer, devil's advocate, technical writer, architect, and scaffolding assistant.

Codex implements.

Lester validates.

The founder remains responsible for decisions.

## Strategic Value

This AI-assisted decision process is a competitive advantage.

It increases iteration speed while preserving founder accountability.

## Working Loop

```text
Founder
    ↓
Idea
    ↓
AI challenges it
    ↓
Founder revises
    ↓
AI challenges again
    ↓
Codex implements
    ↓
Founder validates
    ↓
Repeat
```
