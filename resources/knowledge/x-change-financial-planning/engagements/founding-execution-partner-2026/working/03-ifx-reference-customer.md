# IFX Reference Customer

## Purpose

Preserve the IFX opportunity as a possible first reference customer for x-change / x-Payout.

## Context

IFX is a money changer outfit owned by the founder's college friend.

It has branches in high-end malls.

This makes IFX a potentially valuable first reference customer because:

- the founder has access to the owner
- the business is real
- the business operates in financial services
- the branches face operational liquidity constraints
- the owner can independently judge whether the product creates value

## Reference Customer Logic

IFX may serve as a trusted reference customer.

The logic is:

"If I cannot convince someone who knows me and owns a relevant operating business, how can I convince people who do not know me?"

This does not mean IFX should buy because of friendship.

It means friendship may open the meeting, but business value must close the sale.

## Reference Customer Ladder

The emerging ladder is:

```text
1 Trusted Reference Customer
    ↓
2 Independent Market Customers
    ↓
1 Institutional Reference
    ↓
Scale
```

This sequence may be more meaningful than simply targeting 3-5 customers without considering reference quality.

## IFX Discovery Questions

The first IFX conversation should be discovery-led.

Possible opening question:

"Pare, may mga araw ba na kailangan ninyong tumanggi sa customer dahil naubusan na kayo ng pesos?"

Alternative:

"Have you ever lost a customer because your branch had already run out of pesos?"

Follow-up questions:

- How often does this happen?
- Why does it happen?
- What do customers usually do?
- Do they come back?
- How much revenue do you estimate is lost?
- Would digital settlement help preserve the transaction?
- Which settlement partners would you trust?
- What customer experience would be acceptable at the counter?
