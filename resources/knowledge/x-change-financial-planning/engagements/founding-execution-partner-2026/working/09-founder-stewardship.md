# Founder Stewardship

## Purpose

Define the non-negotiable stewardship standard for accepting Claire's possible ₱1.5M participation.

## Non-Negotiable

If the ₱1.5M investment would put a wedge between Mike and Claire, or between Mike and Lester, Lester would not ask Claire to invest.

## Decision Hierarchy

```text
Relationships
    ↓
Project
    ↓
Funding
```

## Stewardship Principle

No commercial opportunity is worth damaging the relationships that made the opportunity possible.

Claire and Mike are contributing trust capital, not only money or introductions.

## Execution Memorandum Standard

The Execution Memorandum should demonstrate disciplined stewardship:

- transparent reporting
- clear scope
- responsible spending
- no hidden surprises
- no moving goalposts
- early communication of setbacks
- major changes discussed before execution

## Practical Meaning

This funding conversation should be governed by honesty, restraint, and responsibility.

The relationship should never be treated as a cushion against poor discipline.
