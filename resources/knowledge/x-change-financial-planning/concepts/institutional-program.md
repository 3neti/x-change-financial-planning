# Institutional Program

An Institutional Program is the core economic unit of the x-change strategy.

It is a concrete deployment inside an institution, such as payroll, disbursement, treasury, or settlement workflows.

Programs matter because they convert institutional relationships into measurable revenue and operational activity.
