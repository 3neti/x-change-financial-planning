# Break-even Institution

A Break-even Institution is an institution whose ongoing program activity is sufficient to cover the cost of serving it.

The concept exists to make unit economics visible at the institutional level, not just at the total-company level.

It helps determine when institutional adoption becomes operationally sustainable.
