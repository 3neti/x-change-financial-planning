# Bridge Revenue

Bridge Revenue is interim revenue that funds execution before the institutional platform matures.

It exists to reduce delivery risk, not to redefine the long-term platform business model.

It should stay analytically separate from platform revenue so the strategy can distinguish short-term cash support from durable recurring economics.
