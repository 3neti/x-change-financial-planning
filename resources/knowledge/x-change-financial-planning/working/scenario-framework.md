# Scenario Framework

## Purpose

Define the staged scenario framework for x-change financial planning.

## Scenario 0 — Proof of Execution / Pre-Incubation

Objective:

Raise and deploy execution capital to produce a successful proof-of-concept path.

Focus:

- DBP proof of concept
- Paynamics R&D-to-adoption path
- Working platform
- Demonstrations
- Reference implementation
- Bridge revenue
- ₱1.5M execution capital recovery logic

Success is not hypergrowth.

Success is proof that the venture deserves incubation.

## Scenario 1 — Incubation

Objective:

Validate that x-change can become operationally sustainable under realistic Philippine operating conditions.

Focus:

- Institutional demand
- Pricing
- Execution capability
- Operational process
- Burn control
- Near break-even cash flow
- Repeatable deployment

## Scenario 2 — Conservative Operations

Objective:

Model minimal institutional adoption while preserving survivability.

Focus:

- Few paying institutions
- Limited active programs
- Controlled hiring
- Practical support capacity
- Low-burn operations

## Scenario 3 — Expected Growth

Objective:

Model realistic institutional adoption after proof of execution and incubation progress.

Focus:

- Several institutional deployments
- Multiple active programs
- Growing recurring revenue
- Increased implementation capacity
- Stronger operational base

## Scenario 4 — Acceleration

Objective:

Model scaling after incubation has proven survival and repeatability.

Focus:

- Rapid institutional onboarding
- Regional expansion
- Additional product offerings
- Increased hiring
- Larger infrastructure investment
- International deployment readiness

## Principle

Acceleration is modeled as the consequence of successful incubation, not as the initial assumption.
