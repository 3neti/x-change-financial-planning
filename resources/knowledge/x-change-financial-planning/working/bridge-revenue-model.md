# Bridge Revenue Model

## Purpose

Define the cash-flow model that supports x-change before institutional platform revenue matures.

## Context

A potential funder has considered providing ₱1.5M in execution capital.

This capital is intended to help produce a successful proof-of-concept path, including DBP and Paynamics-related validation.

The funder also needs to understand how the capital may be recovered when larger funding arrives and how interim cash flow may be generated while waiting.

## Bridge Revenue

Bridge Revenue exists to finance execution.

It is not the long-term platform business model.

Examples may include:

- Implementation consulting
- Integration services
- Custom software work
- Paid pilots
- White-label demonstrations
- AI consulting
- Enterprise automation
- Government digitization support

## Platform Revenue

Platform Revenue exists to scale the x-change infrastructure business.

Examples may include:

- Institutional licensing
- Implementation fees
- Recurring support contracts
- Settlement instruction fees
- Treasury services
- AI services
- Compliance services
- Future platform modules

## Rule

Bridge Revenue and Platform Revenue must remain analytically separate.

Bridge Revenue reduces execution risk.

Platform Revenue proves the scalable business model.
