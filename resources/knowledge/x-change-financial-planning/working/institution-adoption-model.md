# Institution Adoption Model

## Purpose

Define the core adoption model for x-change financial planning.

## Core Insight

x-change should not be modeled primarily around geography, retail users, wallet users, OFWs, or beneficiary counts.

Those are deployment surfaces.

The business model is institutional adoption.

## Planning Hierarchy

```text
Institution
    ↓
Program
    ↓
Beneficiaries
    ↓
Settlement Instructions
    ↓
Platform Revenue
    ↓
Cash Flow
```

## Institution Types

Examples include:

- Banks
- Government agencies
- Electronic Money Issuers
- Payroll providers
- NGOs
- Cooperatives
- Remittance companies
- Treasury operators
- Settlement providers
- Large enterprises

## Active Institutional Programs

A single institution may deploy multiple programs.

Therefore, the key metric is not merely institution count.

A stronger metric is:

```text
Active Institutional Programs
```

## Implications

- Institution acquisition creates the relationship.
- Program activation creates the economics.
- Existing institutions may expand into additional programs.
- Geography becomes a deployment parameter.
- Users are reached through institutions that already serve them.
