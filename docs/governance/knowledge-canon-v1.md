# Knowledge Canon v1.0

> Canonical principles for structuring and maintaining this knowledge repository.

## Principles

1. Strategy precedes spreadsheets.
2. Financial projections must be derived from an explicit operating strategy.
3. Institutional adoption, not geography, is the primary planning axis.
4. Programs, not raw user counts, are the primary economic unit.
5. Bridge revenue and platform revenue must remain analytically separate.
6. Generated outputs are not the source of truth.
7. Markdown, YAML, CSV, and committed source assets are the durable knowledge artifacts.
8. Assumptions must be explicit, reviewable, and version-controlled.
9. Scenario models must preserve realism under Philippine operating conditions.
10. Acceleration is modeled as the consequence of successful incubation, not as the starting assumption.
