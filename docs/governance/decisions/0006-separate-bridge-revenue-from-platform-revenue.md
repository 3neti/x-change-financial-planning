# 0006 — Separate Bridge Revenue from Platform Revenue

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The current venture stage includes a need to raise execution capital to complete proof-of-concept work, especially for DBP and Paynamics-related validation.

A potential funder has considered providing ₱1.5M but needs to understand how the money may be recovered when larger funding arrives and how interim cash flow may be generated through practical market activity.

This creates two different revenue categories:

1. Bridge Revenue
2. Platform Revenue

Mixing these categories would distort the financial model.

## Decision

Keep Bridge Revenue and Platform Revenue analytically separate.

Bridge Revenue exists to finance execution before institutional platform revenue matures.

Platform Revenue exists to scale the long-term x-change infrastructure business.

## Consequences

- Consulting, implementation services, paid pilots, integration work, and tactical enterprise automation may be modeled as bridge revenue.
- Licensing, settlement fees, recurring support, treasury services, AI services, and platform modules may be modeled as platform revenue.
- Bridge revenue should reduce execution risk but should not define the long-term business model.
- The ₱1.5M execution capital recovery logic should be modeled separately from long-term institutional adoption.
